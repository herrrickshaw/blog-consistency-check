FCI storage scenario — from 851 LMT to the 2,150 LMT plan

← All Articles (Index)

India's grain-storage system has grown slowly (851→918 LMT, FCI+State, 2018→2025) while central-pool stock swings roughly 300–910 LMT with the crop cycle (it touched the ~907 LMT record cited below). The World's Largest Grain Storage Plan (2023, ₹1 lakh cr) now adds +700 LMT of cooperative (PACS) capacity to lift total agri-storage from 1,450 to 2,150 LMT. Each figure is PIB-sourced (below).

FCI + State covered+CAP (LMT) + cooperative / total agri-storage (plan) peak central-pool stock (LMT) 2,150 LMT target
The scenario story: in mid-2021 stock hit a record ~907 LMT — brushing the covered-godown ceiling and spilling into open CAP/hired space (FCI is 70% hired, only ~147 LMT owned — note that 147 LMT is a small share of FCI's total covered capacity, closer to 60% hired by that arithmetic; the 70% figure may use a different capacity base not stated here). The cooperative plan fixes this at the village level (PACS godowns, 1,000 MT each; 2,000 MT hubs) — turning rented, centralised, over-stretched storage into owned, distributed capacity that doubles as procurement centres, Fair Price Shops and processing units — the feedstock node for the biorefinery.
